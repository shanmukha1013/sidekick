# Overview

Finding part-time jobs should be simple.

SideKick helps students discover part-time opportunities while making it easier for employers to find the right candidates.

The platform is designed to make job searching faster, easier, and more accessible for students looking to gain experience and earn while they learn.

---

# Why I Built SideKick

Many students struggle to find flexible part-time jobs that fit their schedules.

I built SideKick to make that process easier by creating a platform where students and employers can connect in one place.

This project also helped me improve my Full-Stack Development skills while building a product that solves a real problem.
